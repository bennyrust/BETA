# Consumption Log photographic pages

Extracted from the ten `log N-M copy.jpg` sheets in the parent directory. Each sheet is read in a snake: top row left to right, then bottom row right to left. The asset filenames and extraction.json retain their original row-major extraction IDs; reading-order.json maps the corrected page numbers to those assets. The HTML follows that mapping, displaying pages 1–80 left to right in eight rows of ten. No handwriting or punch holes were generated. Original paper colour, six punch rims, and photographic detail are retained; background outside the paper and inside its punches is masked to transparency. `extraction.json` records source order and measured corners.

Full images are lossless WebP; small grid thumbnails use the same alpha mask. The existing 8-row, 10-column grid and interactions are preserved. Original `individual pages` assets are retained.
